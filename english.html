💡 How gachapon works (quick tips)

Price and payment: check the price and accepted payment method on the machine before turning it. Payment options vary by machine and store.

Hours and access: shopping-centre hours, store hours and temporary closures may differ. Check the operator or facility's current notice before travelling.

Capsules: follow the store's instructions for opening products and returning empty capsules. Collection rules vary by location.

Language: the rest of this site is in Japanese, but it works well with your browser's translate function. Every store page has an address and a map, which work in any language.
